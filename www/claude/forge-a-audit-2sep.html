2. The gaps, and what happened to each

Gap 1. Data-complete flag wrong on a fresh import: FIXED

The cut-off came from the store's newest period before the batch landed, so a first import marked every row incomplete (813 of 913 high-volume weekly rows, back to 2024). Now the batch's own newest period counts. A fresh-import assertion is in the battery. Rebuilt store: only rows from 27 July to 31 Aug 2026 are incomplete, which is the 30-day lag window.

Gap 2. Window and volume footer vanished silently: HONEST NOW, not complete

Of 64 open findings, 45 had no window and 36 no volume, and the footer said nothing. It now prints "window not recorded" or "volume not recorded". Still open: the evaluators themselves (K7, G2, W2, K1 and others) need to declare a window, and K1, K7 and W2 declare volume as spend, which the store has no column for.

Gap 3. Persistence constants: TRIED, REVERTED, FILED

The engine takes the larger of the evaluator's hard-coded 3 or 6 and the observed streak, so the watch tier was never reached. Deleting the constants made the watch tier real (46 watch observations) but turned three suites red: the batch exam, doc-07 and one contract. Single-run exams, and doc 02 section 5, read a rule's window depth as "periods outside normal".

Constants restored. The right fix is a measured count inside each evaluator's window, the way X2 already counts consecutive falls, not a literal. Whether first-sight window depth counts as persistence is a question for Bernardo and is on the board.

Gap 4. Last hard-coded pound sign in the engine: FIXED

The snooze-expiry note now reads the account's currency. Two secondary chart pages still hard-code pounds and were left alone.

Gap 5. Stale prose: REFRESHED

COMPLIANCE.md (14 suites, the 1 Sept builds no longer "need real usage"), the falsification ledger's tail, README's TESTING.md line, the living board's honestly-open list, and two "plain pounds" comments.
